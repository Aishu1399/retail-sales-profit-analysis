# Retail Sales & Profit Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview
An end-to-end data analytics project using Excel, SQL Server, and Power BI to analyze retail sales, profitability, product performance, customer segments, and regional trends.

## Business Objectives
- Monitor overall sales, profit, and profit margin.
- Identify top-selling products and products with the lowest profit.
- Compare sales and profitability across regions and categories.
- Analyze monthly sales trends for 2024 and 2025.
- Understand customer segment performance.

## Tools & Technologies
- Microsoft Excel
- Microsoft SQL Server
- Power Query
- Power BI
- DAX

## Dataset
- 5,000 sales transactions
- 500 customers
- 20 products
- Reporting period: 2024–2025

This is a sample portfolio dataset, not actual company sales data.

## Project Workflow
1. **Excel:** Data cleaning, validation, XLOOKUP, and PivotTable analysis.
2. **SQL Server:** Aggregations, GROUP BY, CTEs, ranking, and business analysis.
3. **Power BI:** Data modeling, calendar table, date relationships, DAX measures, and interactive dashboards.

## Key Business Insights
- Furniture generated the highest category sales, approximately ₹59 lakh.
- The West region led in both sales and profit.
- Office Chair was the highest-selling product.
- Consumer customers generated the highest sales among customer segments.
- The overall sample dataset recorded a 21.32% profit margin.
